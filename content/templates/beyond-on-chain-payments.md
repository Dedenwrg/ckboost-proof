# Beyond On-Chain Payments

Author: UNSEAL Editorial
Category: Blockchain

Not every digital purchase needs its own blockchain transaction.

<!-- paywall -->

Payment channels allow participants to exchange value through updates to a shared channel state. Opening and closing channels still involve the blockchain, but individual payments can take place off-chain.

For publishers, this creates a way to experiment with small, direct payments for individual articles. The reader pays for a specific piece of work rather than a subscription. Channel liquidity and routing fees remain important practical constraints.
