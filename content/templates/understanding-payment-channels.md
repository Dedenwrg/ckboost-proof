# Understanding Payment Channels

Author: UNSEAL Editorial
Category: Technology

Payment channels move repeated exchanges away from individual on-chain settlement.

<!-- paywall -->

A channel commits funds that participants can use for off-chain payments. A network of channels can route payments between parties that do not share a direct channel.

Available balances determine which directions can carry a payment. A funded channel is not necessarily able to receive funds immediately. Checking inbound and outbound liquidity is an essential part of testing a payment application.
