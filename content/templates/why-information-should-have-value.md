# Why Information Should Have Value

Author: UNSEAL Editorial
Category: Opinion

Digital distribution is inexpensive, but producing useful information takes work.

<!-- paywall -->

Research, editing and verification require time. Direct payments give readers a way to reward the specific work they find useful.

An encrypted publishing model can connect access to payment, but it cannot guarantee that an article is accurate or valuable. Publishers still need to earn trust through clear descriptions and reliable previews.
