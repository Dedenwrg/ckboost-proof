# The Economics of Micropayments

Author: UNSEAL Editorial
Category: Research

Small payments offer an alternative to recurring subscriptions.

<!-- paywall -->

A subscription bundles access to many articles into one recurring charge. A micropayment instead attaches a price to a particular item. This can help occasional readers support writers without committing to a monthly plan.

The model depends on low transaction costs and a simple checkout. If the effort of paying exceeds the value of an article, readers may leave. A clear preview and predictable price can reduce that friction.
