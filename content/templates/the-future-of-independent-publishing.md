# The Future of Independent Publishing

Author: UNSEAL Editorial
Category: Creator Economy

Independent writers benefit from flexible ways to charge for their work.

<!-- paywall -->

Pay-per-article publishing gives writers an alternative to advertising and subscriptions. A reader can inspect a preview and decide whether the full article is worth purchasing.

Encryption can protect the premium text until a payment reveals the required key. This is not digital rights management: once a reader receives plaintext, they can copy it. The goal is a transparent exchange, not permanent control over information.
